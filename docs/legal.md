# Law and consent

- **Someone else's voice:** clone a voice only with its owner's consent – a voice is personal data and,
  in many countries, a personal right.
- **Children's voices:** record and use them only with a parent's or guardian's consent and keep
  everything local. Chatterbox runs entirely on the machine; ElevenLabs sends text and voice to its
  cloud.
- **Cartoon and film characters:** the recordings are copyrighted and the actor has rights to the voice
  and the performance. Keep such voices private: do not publish the model or samples and never use them
  commercially.
- **Model licences:** Chatterbox is MIT. XTTS-v2 is CPML (non-commercial) – treat voices from its clips
  as non-commercial too. Chatterbox adds an inaudible watermark (Resemble Perth).
- **Labelling:** if you ever publish synthetic speech, mark it as AI-generated (EU AI Act, art. 50).
- **Private data** (recordings, datasets, models) stays in the data folder on the machine, outside the
  repository.

This is not legal advice.
